# Public landing page design acceptance

- The root URL (`/`) must open the public marketing site without authentication.
- The header must expose Why Mod Alpha, Features, How it works, Pricing, FAQ, Login and Get Started.
- Public content must include hero, product features, the five-stage animated explainer, pricing, FAQs and disclosures.
- The public explainer must show only the high-level process: Market Data Collection, AI-Assisted Analysis, Monthly SIP Date, Subscriber Notifications, You Stay in Control.
- Do not disclose proprietary algorithm details, scoring thresholds, factor weights, parameters or model architecture.
- Animation starts when the explainer enters the viewport, reveals stages in order, animates connectors and inner visual elements, and offers a replay control.
- Respect reduced-motion settings by showing all stages immediately without motion.
- User/admin guest portals remain separate from the public marketing site.
- Visual reference: `assets/screenshots/07_latest_visual_reference.png` and the approved How Mod Alpha Works concept in `assets/screenshots/06_how_mod_alpha_works.png`.
